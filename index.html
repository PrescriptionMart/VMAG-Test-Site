About VMAG Bridge City

A team that understands the mission — because we’ve lived it

VMAG stands for the Veterans Medical Assistance Group. We are a veteran-owned and operated organization dedicated to helping fellow veterans make sense of the complex VA disability rating and compensation system.

Too many veterans walk away from benefits they earned simply because the paperwork is confusing, the medical language is dense, or no one ever explained how their conditions connect to their service. We’re here to change that. The licensed medical professionals at VMAG review your records, help you understand your current condition, and make sure your claim accurately reflects the service-connected conditions you live with.

As your local Bridge City branch, we’re proud to serve veterans across Orange County and Southeast Texas with face-to-face, neighbor-to-neighbor care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for veterans

What does VMAG stand for?

VMAG stands for the Veterans Medical Assistance Group. We help veterans understand their medical conditions, how those conditions connect to their military service, and the VA disability benefits they may be eligible to receive.

How much does it cost?

Your initial consultation is free. After that, we charge an affordable, transparent flat rate — never a percentage of your back pay. We’ll go over current pricing for physical and mental health claim support during your consultation so you always know the cost up front.

Do you guarantee I’ll get a higher rating?

No one can honestly guarantee a specific VA outcome. What we can promise is honest guidance, a thorough review of your records by licensed professionals, and help making sure your claim accurately reflects your service-connected conditions.

Are you part of the VA?

No. VMAG is a private organization and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government agency. We’re an independent team of veterans and licensed professionals who help you navigate the process.

Who conducts the medical evaluations?

All medical professionals who conduct evaluations at VMAG are licensed to do so. They review your records and help document the conditions relevant to your claim.

How do I get started?

It’s simple — book a free consultation below, or call or text us at (256) 645-8168. We’ll take it from there.
